Treat this link like a key. Anyone who has it can read your emergency information without a password — that is what makes it work when you cannot type one. Do not post it publicly. If a tag is lost, issue a new link below.

Writing it to an NFC tag

  1. Get a writable tag

    Any NTAG213 or NTAG215 sticker, wristband or card will do. They cost very little and hold far more than this link needs.

  2. Install a tag writer

    NFC Tools works on both Android and iPhone. Any app that writes a URL record is fine.

  3. Write the link as a URL record

    Choose "Write", then "URL", then paste the address above. Hold the tag against the back of your phone until it confirms.

  4. Test it before you rely on it

    Scan it with a different phone, ideally one that is not signed in here, and check it shows what you expect.

  5. Consider locking the tag

    Most writers can make a tag read-only. That stops anyone overwriting your link, but it cannot be undone — do it only once you are sure.
