If someone is having a seizure now

Note the time. Move hard or sharp objects away and cushion their head. Do not restrain them and do not put anything in their mouth. Once the movements stop, turn them onto their side. Stay with them until they are fully alert.

Call an ambulance if

The seizure passes five minutes, another follows immediately, they are injured, they are in water, they are pregnant, they do not regain consciousness, or it is their first seizure.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
